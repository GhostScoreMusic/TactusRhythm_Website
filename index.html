Speed of entry

A passage in seconds, not minutes.

Tap the meter keyboard. Each tap adds a bar, 5/8, 7/16, 4/4, 3/8, in any order, landing with correct accents by default. Enter any custom meter too, any numerator from 1 to 32 over 2, 4, 8, or 16. For the bars that need more, every subdivision is a tap in the editor.

Ableton Link

Everyone stays locked to a shared tempo grid.

Ableton Link starts and stops Tactus in sync with another Tactus user, or any Link-enabled device, on the same network, each still following their own accents and subdivisions.

Starting together is automatic. Staying together through a rit or accel means everyone needs the same tempo map, so Tactus lets you share a passage and import the whole thing, or just its tempo map. At a steady tempo, matching BPM is all it takes.

Tempo shape editor

Draw rits and accels the way you hear them.

Draw a tempo curve across any range of bars, linear, ease-in, or ease-out per segment. A ritardando that lands softly. An accelerando that plateaus before the downbeat.

Everything else.

  • Die-face dot clusters Read each beat as a die face, not a count.
  • Four accent states Strong, normal, soft, silent, each its own click.
  • Subdivision independence Split any beat 2–9 ways, then subdivide again for nested rhythms.
  • Tuplet grouping Group consecutive beats into a tuplet, like 3 notes over 2.
  • Conducted feel Overlay a conductor’s beat pattern on any bar.
  • Practice speed Slow the whole passage, 50–125%, in one tap.
  • Count-in Plays the previous bar's meter as a lead-in.
  • iPad, iPhone & Android Native on iOS, synced through iCloud. Android coming soon.
  • .rhythmmap export Share a passage in a single file.

Frequently asked

Questions, in plain language.

Can the tempo change within a passage?
Yes, two ways: per-bar BPM overrides, or the Tempo Shape editor for a gradual ramp across a range of bars.
Does it sync between my iPad and iPhone?
Yes, passages sync via iCloud and appear on all your devices automatically. Nothing beyond your Apple ID required.
Is there a subscription?
No. One-time purchase, everything included, free updates for the lifetime of the major version.
Can I share passages with collaborators?
Yes, export a .rhythmmap file and send it any way you like. They open it directly in their copy of Tactus and see exactly the map you built.
How do I import a passage or a tempo map?
Opening a .rhythmmap file adds it to your Library as a whole new passage. To bring in just the bar structure and tempo instead, use Import Tempo Map in a passage's settings and pick another passage. It copies over the meters and tempos, padding or trimming bars to match, while keeping the beat content you already have.
Is there an Android version?
Not yet, it's coming. Ableton Link sync will work the same way between iPhone, iPad, and Android once it ships.

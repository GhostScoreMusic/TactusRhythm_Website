## Overall Impression

Strong product-fidelity work undercut by two issues: mobile hero shows zero legible content before the fold, and the interactive demo — the page's best proof-of-craft — has a live rendering bug (sheet bleed-through). Fix those before adding anything new.

## What's Working

1. The interactive "Try it" demo is a genuine working port of the app's own logic, placed right after the hero.
2. Typography-as-product-fidelity: fonts chosen to literally match the shipping Android app.
3. Restrained accent economy: one teal accent carries all active/CTA states against near-monochrome.
